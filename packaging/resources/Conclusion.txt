The driver is installed.

To add your printer:
  1. Connect the iP100 by USB and turn it on.
  2. Open System Settings > Printers & Scanners and click Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax.
  3. Select the iP100, then under "Use" choose "Select Software..." and pick "Canon iP100 Native (unofficial driver)".
  4. Click Add.

If colours print with bands or gaps, the print head needs cleaning. With plain paper loaded:
  - To print a nozzle check page, hold the printer's RESUME/CANCEL button until the power light flashes twice, then release.
  - To clean the print head, hold RESUME/CANCEL until the power light flashes once, then release.
Repeat the cleaning until the nozzle check page shows no gaps.

To uninstall, run uninstall.sh from the project's GitHub page with sudo.
